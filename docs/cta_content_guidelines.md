# Page+CTA Template Content Guidelines

> [!NOTE]
> **Disclaimer:** The headings, sub-headlines, and button labels provided below are **examples only**. The actual content should be tailored to fit the specific context and narrative of each page.

**Purpose:** To define the content strategy for the "Next Step" CTA section appended to the bottom of inner pages.
**Goal:** To guide users towards **Histare's Services** and specific offerings, ensuring they can easily navigate to the relevant service page.

---

## Strategy: "Explore Related Services"
**Context:** Narrative or History pages where the content naturally leads to a specific service offering (e.g., reading about *History* leads to *Heritage Restoration*).

### Content Requirements
*   **Headline:** "Preserving Legacies" or "Expert Care for Your Collection"
*   **Sub-headline:** Connect the current topic to a specific service. (e.g., "Discover how our restoration experts breathe new life into history.")
*   **Primary Button:**
    *   **Label:** [Name of Service] (e.g., "View Restoration Services")
    *   **Action:** Navigates to the specific Service page.
*   **Visual:** An image representing the service in action (e.g., a restoration in progress).
